# Product

A local-first spaced repetition study application. Users create decks of cards
and review them on a spaced schedule to study more effectively over time.

## Key Characteristics

- **Local-first**: data and study state live on the user's device rather than a
  remote backend. Favor client-side persistence and offline-capable flows.
- **Study-focused**: the core loop is creating decks/cards and reviewing them
  according to a spaced repetition schedule.

## Current State

Early-stage scaffold. The project structure, styling, and tooling are in place;
the deck and review features are yet to be built.
